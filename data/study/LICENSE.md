# Study data licences

The files in this directory are data, not app code. They are licensed separately from the
app (see the repository `LICENSE`) under the licences below. They are generated by
`scripts/import-study.py`; the per-day files in `public/study/` are generated from them by
`scripts/build-study.js` and carry the same licences.

Changes made to every source: extraction of the fields listed, Unicode whitespace
normalisation, removal of USFM markup and lexicon HTML, re-keying by USFM verse IDs, and
(in `public/study/`) splitting by reading day. No Scripture wording was changed.

New Testament, with their positions in the Van Dyck Arabic text.

- Source: TTAraSVD — Translation Tags for Arabic SVD, version 4.0.1, STEPBible.org.
  STEPBible-Data commit `1f3423d42400f59f1f30fe08f74e38fcd3bbf7bc`.
- The original work by STEPBible.org, and Arabic Bible Outreach Ministry, is available for
  free at "STEPBible Arabic Bibles"
  https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data/tree/master/Tagged-Bibles/Arabic%20Bibles
- Arabic text digitisation, formatting and diacritics by "Arabic Bible Outreach Ministry"
  (الخدمة العربية للكرازة بالانجيل), https://www.arabicbible.com/. Greek data from the
  Translators Amalgamated Greek NT — STEPBible.org, CC BY 4.0.
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Changes: kept only words present in the Textus Receptus edition column; word positions

  without a Greek word dropped.
